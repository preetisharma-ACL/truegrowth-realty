Project Description

M3M The Line is a premium mixed-use development located in Sector 72, Noida, designed to combine high-street retail, food and entertainment spaces with studio-style/pentsuite units. Developed by M3M, the project is positioned as a contemporary commercial and lifestyle destination in Central Noida. Its strategic location provides convenient access to the Sector 51 Metro Station and major road networks, while the surrounding residential catchment supports strong visibility and potential customer footfall.

Spread across approximately 3.04–3.05 acres, the development is planned around modern commercial infrastructure, attractive frontage, retail zones, dining areas and lifestyle amenities. Project information highlights features such as double-height retail spaces, dedicated F&B areas, entertainment zones, high-speed elevators, power backup, CCTV surveillance, parking and landscaped areas.

The project is RERA registered under UPRERAPRJ246070 and is currently under construction, with possession scheduled for July 2028. Available inventory reported by property sources includes retail shops and studio/pentsuite units, with studio sizes commonly listed between 865 and 1,205 sq.ft.
